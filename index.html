Why it wins

Same price as a static board. Five displays in one.

Paper is free until someone swaps it, it fades, or a number has to be reprinted. Static LED boards need one board per method. Dytrix removes all of it with one button.

One board, five displays

No five separate boards or five pieces of paper for five payment methods. One device sits on the counter and shows whichever mode is in use.

Up to 5 accounts per mode

Under each of the five categories, the board holds up to five different accounts. One board covers all your numbers — no extra blinking LED boards.

Button, not paper

Staff switch modes in a second. No printing, no removing stickers, no faded numbers, no reprint runs when a number changes.

Cannot be swapped

The number shown is digital. A criminal cannot peel it off and replace it with their own — the number on screen is the real number.

Plug and play

USB Type-C powered from any normal phone charger. Wall-mounted with the bracket or placed on the counter — ready in minutes.

Professional by default

One consistent, clean display at every counter. No faded papers, no mismatched boards — and one standard device for procurement.

The fraud angle: paper numbers fade, fall off and get swapped. A digital display can't be peeled. One prevented fraud pays for the board.

How it works

From box to live counter in an afternoon.

01

Set up once

Provide the numbers for each of the Our Products — up to 5 accounts per mode. That's the only setup.

02

Mount or place

Hang it on the wall with the bracket, or sit it on the counter where the payment happens.

03

Power up

Connect the USB cable to the Type-C port and plug into any normal phone charger.

04

Press the top button

Each press moves to the next mode — M-PESA, Paybill, Pochi, Send Money, Till — and the display updates instantly.
